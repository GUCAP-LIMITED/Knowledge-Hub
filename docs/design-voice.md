# Voice & microcopy

How UAPP Academy sounds. The product is a workplace learning hub — the voice is a
**knowledgeable colleague**: clear, warm, and to the point. It respects the reader's time
and never shows off.

## Voice in three words

- **Clear** — plain words over clever ones. If a nine-year-old wouldn't get it, rewrite it.
- **Warm** — encouraging, never cold or bureaucratic. We're on the learner's side.
- **Concise** — say it once, in the fewest words that stay friendly. Cut throat-clearing.

## Rules

1. **Sentence case** for buttons, form labels, body copy, toasts, and empty states. Navigation
   entries and page titles keep **Title Case** by established convention ("Team Progress",
   "My Certificates") — that's the one deliberate exception. Never ALL CAPS as copy (small-caps
   section labels are a visual style, not text).
2. **Active voice, present tense.** "Start course", not "Course can be started".
3. **Lead with the verb on actions.** Buttons are `Verb [noun]`: "Add type", "View certificate",
   "Take the quiz". Never "Click here" or a bare noun.
4. **Second person.** Talk to "you"; the app is "we" only when owning an action.
5. **No jargon or system-speak.** "Something went wrong" beats "Error 500". Say what happened
   and what to do next.
6. **Empty states encourage, they don't apologise.** "No certificates yet — complete a course to
   earn one", not "You have no data".
7. **Distinguish empty from filtered.** "No courses yet" (nothing exists) vs "No courses match"
   (a search/filter hid them) — always tell the reader which.
8. **Numbers and specifics build trust.** "Expires in 61 days" beats "Expires soon". "3 of 3 done"
   beats "Almost there".
9. **Punctuation is light.** One exclamation mark per moment, max — save it for genuine
   celebration ("You passed!"). Use the curly apostrophe (’), an em dash (—) for asides.
10. **Toasts state the outcome.** "Course deleted", "Review submitted" — past tense, no "successfully".

## Quick reference

| Instead of…           | Write…                                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| "Submit"              | "Submit for review" / "Save changes"       |
| "No data"             | "No submissions yet"                       |
| "Error occurred"      | "Couldn't load courses — try again"        |
| "Are you sure?"       | "Delete this course? This can't be undone" |
| "Click to view"       | "View certificate"                         |
| "Successfully saved!" | "Saved"                                    |

When in doubt, read it aloud. If it sounds like a form letter, rewrite it until it sounds like
a helpful colleague. See also [`docs/design-accents.md`](./design-accents.md) and
[`docs/design-motion.md`](./design-motion.md).
